The Mech Model

Predictive power ratings and game lines for every men's and women's Division I college basketball game. Each team is rated on offense, defense and tempo, adjusted for opponent, with a roster-based preseason start, conference strength, and team-specific home court. Lines are generated and logged before tip-off every morning, and every line is graded against the final score and the Vegas spread, with nothing edited after the fact.

For entertainment and informational purposes only. Not betting advice. Lines are model estimates and carry real uncertainty; past results don't guarantee future accuracy. Not affiliated with the NCAA, any conference, school, or sportsbook. Game data from ESPN via sportsdataverse; Vegas lines are DraftKings numbers as shown on ESPN.
